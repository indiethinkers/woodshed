What it is

Woodshed is a desktop-first knowledge management application. You pick a folder on your computer. Woodshed makes that folder feel like a single workspace for everything you think about and everyone you know. Each document is a plain .md file you can open in any editor.

How it works

Type [[Jamie Parker]] in your daily journal and Woodshed creates a person page for him. Mention #task and a row appears in a tag table that auto-aggregates across the whole vault. Connect Gmail and Woodshed pulls Jamie Parker's recent emails onto his page, read-only, indexed locally. Connect calendar and his next meeting shows up too.

Wikilinks are the graph. Tags are the database. People are the nodes. Your daily journal is the gravity well. The whole thing is files in a folder you control.

Where your data lives

On your computer. There is no Woodshed server. Your markdown files stay in your vault folder. The rebuildable local index lives in Woodshed's app data directory. Gmail App Passwords use an owner-only local file; calendar secrets use your operating system's credential store. Nothing transmits to anyone we operate, because we do not operate anything. The privacy policy walks through this in detail.

Who it's for

People who already pay for Notion or Superhuman or Notion Calendar and find themselves wishing they spoke to each other. People who like Obsidian's markdown discipline but can't get past the aesthetic. People who manage other people, or relationships, or clients, and want their software to treat people as first-class.
